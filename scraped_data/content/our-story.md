## Our Story 

1994 

Commencement of Texturising 

- Initiated DTY manufacturing and texturising. 
- Expanded production with DT machines. 
- 2010: Installed nylon FDY spinning plant. 

2011 

The Nylon FDY Plant 

The Nylon FDY plant was established under the name “Salasar Polytex Pvt Ltd.” 

2014 

Commencement of Operations 

- Launched operations in warp knitting net manufacturing. 
- Established a foundation for growth through commitment to quality and customer satisfaction. 

2015 

Transforming and Expanding in the Warp Knitting Industry 

- Became an innovative leader in the warp knitted fabrics market with cutting-edge solutions. 
- Differentiated through a customer-centric approach and advanced technology. 
- Achieved significant growth, especially in 4M EL, by adopting state-of the-art technology. 
- Maintained industry leadership by effectively meeting diverse customer demands. 

2017 

Strategic Expansion, Technological Investments & Entry into New Markets 

- Invested in advanced Raschel and Circular Knitted machines, enhancing efficiency and product range. 
- 2016: Expanded into the kids' wear segment, showcasing versatility and market adaptability. 
- Entered the curtain segment, applying warp knitting expertise to the interior design industry. 
- 2021: Established a state-of-the-art production unit to meet growing market demands. 

2021 

Harnessing Renewable Energy 

- We acknowledged the significance of sustainability and social  responsibility. 
- We took a major step by installing solar plants. 
- Our goal was to reduce our carbon footprint. 
- We aimed to contribute to a greener and more sustainable future. 

2024 

Evolving Through Continuous Improvement 

- We entered the technical textile sector, supplying glass fiber fabric to the composite market. 
- Our commitment to innovation is central to our values and growth strategy. 
- We focus on continuous improvement to provide top-quality products and services. 
- Our goal is to meet India’s evolving needs 

Providing innovative textile solutions with outstanding quality and a strong focus on customer needs.
