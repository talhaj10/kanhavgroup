## Our Specialisations 

- 

## Warp-Knitted Textiles 

Our expertise in warp-knitted textiles allows us to deliver high-performance fabrics that
meet the exacting standards of various industries. Utilizing state-of-the-art German
machinery, our advanced facility in Surat, Gujarat, produces textiles known for their
durability, flexibility, and superior quality. Whether for industrial applications, fashion, or
technical textiles, our warp-knitted fabrics are designed to perform under the most
demanding conditions. 

- 

## Nylon FDY Yarns 

Kanhav Industries is also at the forefront of nylon FDY (Fully Drawn Yarn) production,
delivering yarns that are synonymous with strength, consistency, and versatility. Our
nylon FDY yarns are used in a wide array of applications, from everyday clothing to high-
tech industrial products. Our commitment to quality ensures that each yarn produced
meets the rigorous demands of our global clientele.
